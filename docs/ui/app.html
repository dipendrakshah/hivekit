What a swap would cost

Moving the worker seat to claude-sonnet takes a morning routine from $0.03 to about $0.34 and lifts first-try accuracy from 88% to ~97%. The retry ladder already covers most of that gap.

Changing a model applies to newly spawned workers. In-flight workers finish on the old one. No restart.

Why receipts exist

Every always-ask action stores the payload or diff, the deciding human, the model that proposed it, and the timestamp. This is the audit answer to the trust gap in hosted agent products: you can go back and see exactly what was done on your behalf, and who said yes.
